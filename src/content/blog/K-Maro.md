Thu, Aug 13, 2026

Listened to K.Maro’s 2025 album “**Place de Loréane (VOL.1)**”… The man turned into Francis Cabrel but Canadian. Love it. 

“**Rêver mieux**” gets a special mention for being strangely poetic. 
“**Whisky on Ice**” is extremely funny to me, but it IS also genuinely touching.  But I still cannot listen to the phrase “_Dans le jardin zen zen… J’ai le coeur en hiver_” without snorting. Oh, and “_Alors en kimono / À genoux je prie / Get me a whisky on ice_”… Pure poetry. Prayer-worthy. Truly.

And his single “**La Reine de la Fête**” makes me feel like that should be a soundtrack for some medieval romance drama. AND it gets extra points for teaching me another piece of useless information as I now know what _“macadam”_ is. _On a pansé nos blessures sur le macadam~_

The character arc from being a “**Million Dollar Boy**” to, 20 years later, a man who is basically a troubadour talking about God, memory, humanity(and whisky, as a tribute to his club days past, I presume).

I wanted nice beats of old K.Maro and got a man questioning humanity(but the beats are still nice). Good enough.
